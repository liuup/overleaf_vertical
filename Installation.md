## Configuration (Optional)

### Adding Custom Domains

If you use cn.overleaf.com or a self-hosted Overleaf instance, you can add them one at a time:

1. Right-click the extension icon in your browser toolbar
2. Select "Options" from the menu
3. In the options page, enter your custom domain pattern in the input field
4. Click "Add Domain" button
5. Repeat steps 3-4 to add more domains
6. Reload your Overleaf page

**Example domain patterns:**

- `*://cn.overleaf.com/*` - Chinese Overleaf
- `*://latex.sysu.edu.cn/*` - Sun Yat-sen University LaTeX
- `*://overleaf.mycompany.com/*` - Company self-hosted instance
- `*://*.overleaf.com/*` - All Overleaf subdomains

### Using the Resizable Divider

When in vertical split mode:

- Click and drag the divider between the editor and PDF preview
- Adjust to your preferred split ratio
- The position will be maintained as you work
