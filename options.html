Configure custom domains for the extension

Active Domains

How to add domains: Enter one domain pattern at a time and click "Add Domain". Repeat to add more.

Pattern Examples:
• *://cn.overleaf.com/* - Chinese Overleaf
• *://latex.sysu.edu.cn/* - Sun Yat-sen University LaTeX
• *://overleaf.mycompany.com/* - Company self-hosted instance
• *://*.overleaf.com/* - All Overleaf subdomains

Note: The default domain *://www.overleaf.com/* cannot be removed. Changes take effect after reloading Overleaf pages.
